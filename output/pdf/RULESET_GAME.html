Premise

THE CENTRAL IDEA

Everything on the screen may be part of the puzzle.

Text. Words. Numbers. Buttons. Instructions. Progress. Windows. Time. Input. Previous levels. Memory. The interface itself.

RULESET is a minimalist puzzle game about assumptions. It is not primarily math, trivia, detective fiction, platforming, combat, 2D, or 3D. It teaches a rule, lets that rule settle into instinct, then asks the player to recognize it somewhere they did not expect.

Phase Encoding

ECHO IS A GAME CIPHER

A phase rotates every token around the same ring.

To encode, move forward by the phase. To decode, move backward. The player experiences this as turning a dial until the language returns to its familiar shape.

encodedIndex =
(index + phase) mod 8

Move forward around the ring.

normalizedIndex =
(encodedIndex - phase + 8) mod 8

Move backward to restore meaning.

DESIGNED FOR PUZZLE PLAY. NOT SECURE REAL-WORLD ENCRYPTION.

RULESET / Spoiler-Free

PUBLIC-FACING DESCRIPTION

A minimalist puzzle game where the screen is not a frame around the puzzle.

It is the puzzle.

RULESET asks players to observe carefully, test assumptions, and learn a growing language of interaction. Every surprise follows a rule. Every solution feels impossible until it feels inevitable.

NO COMBAT / NO TRIVIA / NO PIXEL HUNTING

Why RULESET Can Grow

CREATOR NOTE

RULESET is not a collection of tricks. It is a way of teaching perception.

That makes expansion possible without erasing the player's intelligence.

Season I teaches that interface conventions are negotiable. Season II teaches that information can be transformed, indexed, remembered, and recombined. Future Seasons can introduce wholly new languages of interaction while respecting both lessons.

The constraint is continuity. New grammar should not invalidate old grammar; it should let old knowledge operate at a new scale. The player should feel more capable at the start of each Season than they did at the start of the last.

A Season can change the medium of thought - space, sound, timing, collaboration, authorship - without changing the contract. Teach clearly. Transform deliberately. Recontextualize rather than repeat.

The long-term promise is not “more puzzles.” It is a growing literacy: the sensation that every solved rule permanently enlarges what the player can imagine doing next.
