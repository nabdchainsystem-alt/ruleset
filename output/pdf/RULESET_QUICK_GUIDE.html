The Core Idea

SURPRISE WITHOUT CHEATING

The puzzle is not only inside the game window.

The whole interface is the puzzle surface.

RULESET is not about obscure knowledge or fast reflexes. It is about interpretation: learning what actions exist, then recognizing when those actions apply somewhere new.
