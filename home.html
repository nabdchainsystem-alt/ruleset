A puzzle game where the interface is the puzzle.

Play

Seventy-five stages. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for. Your progress is kept in this browser.

Everything is fair game

The words. The buttons. The number in the corner. The size of your window. If it is on the screen, it may be part of the puzzle — including the parts that look like ordinary software.

Read the instruction carefully

Every stage tells you exactly what to do. Doing exactly that is rarely enough. Nothing here is a trick for its own sake: once you see it, it was always there.

Two seasons

The first teaches you how this thing thinks. The second assumes you were paying attention. There is a way through every stage, and a hint waiting if you want one.

Start at the first stage

Built with no frameworks and no build step — it is a folder of HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
